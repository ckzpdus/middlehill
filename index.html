Middle Hill Uploader

Middle Hill Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of The Middle Hill, a YouTube channel of sing-along songs for toddlers.

It runs on the owner's own computer and publishes the channel's own finished videos:

  • to the owner's YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API (video, title, description, tags, translated titles and descriptions, captions, thumbnail, playlist and scheduled publish time);
  • to the owner's own Instagram professional account, as Reels, through the Instagram Platform API.

The tool is not offered to anyone else, and no other user can sign in to it.
