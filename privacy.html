Privacy Policy — Middle Hill Uploader

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Middle Hill Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Middle Hill to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube: the owner's channel name and ID, the titles and IDs of the channel's uploaded videos (to avoid uploading a video twice), the metadata, caption tracks and playlists of the channel's own videos, and the channel's playlists. It uploads videos, captions and thumbnails and edits the metadata of the channel's own videos.
  • Instagram / Facebook: the ID of the owner's own Instagram professional account and the linked Facebook Page, used to publish the owner's own videos as Reels.

The tool does not access viewers' data, comments, messages, subscribers' personal information, or any account other than the owner's.

What is stored, and where

  • Sign-in tokens issued by Google and Meta, stored only in a file on the owner's own computer.
  • A local log of uploaded video IDs, publish times and upload dates, stored on the same computer.

Nothing is stored on any server run by the tool. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Sharing

The tool sends data only to YouTube (Google) and Instagram (Meta) to publish the owner's own content. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone else.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Instagram and Facebook access can be removed in the account's apps and websites settings.

When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner's computer. The local upload log is deleted on request, within 7 days.

Meta

Instagram and Facebook data is handled under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the owner through the channel's About page.
